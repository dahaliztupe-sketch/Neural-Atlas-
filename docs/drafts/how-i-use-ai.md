## EN
I am a first-year student and a self-taught programmer. I use AI tools to build, and I say so on every project that was built this way.

- Neural Atlas: I decide the content and review it. AI tools helped with the code.
- WANASBRAND: the code started with Google AI Studio. Then I fixed and improved it.
- Kalmeron-Two: built with the help of AI. It is an experiment, and parts of it are not enabled.

What I do myself: I choose what to build, I read and review the code, I fix what breaks, and I write the labels that say what is real. I am learning C++ (finished the Elzero track, no certificate), I started Python, and I am working through CS50x.

What I do not claim: that I wrote this code alone, or that these projects have users or customers.
